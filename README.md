<summary><strong>Bananota</strong> · Building · 2026</summary>

Indie hacker product focused on helping people discover, share and evaluate supermarket offers while building a community-driven source of local price information.


**Problem**  
Supermarket prices, promotions and product availability are fragmented across different stores, making it difficult for consumers to compare opportunities and know whether an offer is actually worth it.


**Approach**  
Build a mobile-first platform where users can discover and publish supermarket offers, interact with the community, maintain shopping lists and receive alerts about relevant products and prices.


**Stack**  
Laravel · PHP · React · TypeScript · Vite · Tailwind CSS · SQLite · PWA

**Selected features**
- Community-driven supermarket offer feed
- Product, brand and category catalog
- Supermarket chains and store locations
- Offer publishing with product and price information
- Likes, comments and community interactions
- Shopping lists
- Internal price and product alerts
- Usage metrics and product analytics
- Mobile-first Progressive Web App

</details>
